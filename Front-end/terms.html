Our Service Terms

Please review these terms before approving a project with Mustapha and Co / MMD PAINTING.

Quotes and project approval

We discuss the work, surfaces, materials, finish, and location before preparing an estimate. An estimate is not a booking; work is scheduled after you approve the written scope and price.

Price and payment

The approved quote or invoice will state the agreed price, currency, payment timing, and any deposit or material costs. No deposit or extra charge is due unless it has been agreed with you.

Schedule and access

We agree a start date and expected schedule with you. Weather, site conditions, or material availability may require a change; we will communicate as soon as reasonably possible. Please arrange the agreed access and clear the work area before we arrive.

Changes and additional work

Work outside the approved scope may affect the price or schedule. We will explain the change and get your agreement before proceeding with additional work.

Materials and preparation

The quote will describe the included materials and preparation. Please confirm chosen colors and finishes before work begins. We will discuss any unexpected surface conditions that could affect the result.

Rescheduling and cancellation

Please contact us promptly if you need to change or cancel a booking. We will discuss any work already completed or costs already agreed with you; no cancellation fee applies unless it was disclosed and accepted in advance.

Completion and concerns

We invite you to review the finished work with us. If you have a concern, contact us promptly with the project details so we can discuss it and agree on appropriate next steps.

Safe work and customer property

Please keep children, visitors, and pets away from active work areas. We take reasonable care to protect nearby property and keep the work area orderly.

Website analytics

We count page views and use a random identifier stored in your browser to estimate unique browsers. Website-analytics records store a hashed identifier, page, and date—not visitor names or raw IP addresses. These estimates count browsers, not exact individual people; clearing browser storage or switching devices can affect the estimate. Only authorized administrators can view the reports. Analytics records are retained for up to 400 days and are used to understand website traffic.

These general terms do not replace or limit any rights you have under applicable consumer law. Project-specific written agreements take precedence where they differ from these general guidelines.
